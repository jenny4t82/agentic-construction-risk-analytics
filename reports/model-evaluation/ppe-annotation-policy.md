# SafeSite PPE Annotation Policy — Stage 1 Draft

**Status:** project annotation policy for human-reviewed dataset work. It is not a legal or regulatory interpretation, and does not replace site-specific rules or a qualified safety professional. The policy does not authorize model/application changes.

## Decision vocabulary

Annotators must distinguish these states and must not force uncertain cases into a violation class:

- **Not visible:** the relevant body region is outside the image, fully occluded, or too small to inspect.
- **Not applicable:** the PPE item is not relevant to the visible task or the applicable site/task policy does not require it.
- **Uncertain:** available visual or task context cannot establish compliant or non-compliant status.
- **Explicit violation:** the relevant region is sufficiently visible and the applicable site/task policy requires the PPE, while the image clearly shows it is absent or improperly worn according to the project-specific codebook.

An absent positive PPE detection is never evidence for a missing-PPE label. Violation labels require visible evidence and an applicable requirement. Reviewers must not infer legal requirements from image appearance alone.

## Annotation and review rules

1. Keep positive gear classes and explicit violation classes separate. A worker with no detected `Hardhat` is not automatically `NO-Hardhat`.
2. Label a violation only with both (a) sufficient visible evidence and (b) a documented project/site/task requirement. If either is unknown, record uncertainty for human review.
3. For multi-person images, each gear/violation box must be attributable to the relevant person and body region. Avoid attaching a PPE label to a nearby person or background object.
4. Use the source dataset's box convention only after it is documented. Do not silently convert boxes around hands, heads, people, or whole workers between conventions.
5. Record sample status as `plausible`, `ambiguous`, `out_of_scope`, `likely_mislabelled`, or `requires_human_review`. These are review classifications, not automatic corrections.
6. The supplement's six previously reviewed `no_gloves` examples remain documented as out-of-domain concerns in [the Phase 2B review](./dataset-integration-readiness.md#no_gloves-semantic-review). They do not prove that every supplementary `no_gloves` annotation is invalid.

## Current support summary

The 25-class checkpoint includes positive PPE classes `Gloves` (1), `Hardhat` (2), `Mask` (4), `Safety Vest` (11), and explicit violation classes `NO-Hardhat` (5), `NO-Mask` (6), `NO-Safety Vest` (7). `NO-Gloves` is absent. The application risk taxonomy recognizes normalized `no_gloves`, but that does not add a prediction class to the checkpoint. The supplementary dataset has `no_gloves` (9) and `no_helmet` (7), but no `no_mask` or `no_vest`; its annotation scope must be reviewed before use.
