## Cross-cutting decisions

- Keep all 25 current class IDs/names unchanged during Stage 1. Do not map `none` to an absence class or map generic names to violations without source-codebook evidence.
- Preserve the original held-out test split. Do not move test images, train on it, or tune thresholds using it.
- Keep all 10 orphan supplementary label files (93 rows) excluded until original associations are verified. Keep the nine known train-to-test scene-family overlaps as a data-leakage gate.
- The supplementary dataset is not ready for integration while source-photo rights, publisher annotation policy, orphan pairing, near-duplicate grouping, and human mapping approval remain unresolved.
- Stage 1 is complete as an audit deliverable. PPE remediation is not complete; no class has been approved for bulk training.

## Stage 2 entry gates

1. Obtain human approval of the annotation policy and any task/site requirement source used for each violation label.
2. Obtain source codebook and written publisher clarification for image provenance and rights, `no_gloves` semantics, orphan pairings, and scene sequences; complete legal review.
3. Build a separate, versioned, file-level eligibility/remap manifest. Keep exact source labels and decisions; do not modify source files.
4. Resolve source/scene grouping before assigning any candidate data to a split. Keep the original test boundary untouched.
5. Review every candidate violating `plausible`/scope criteria and maintain an `uncertain` state until a human resolves it.
